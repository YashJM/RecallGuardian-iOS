## Description
RecallGuardian puts every Canadian safety recall in one place — ranked by how much it matters to your household, written in plain language, and always linked to the official Government of Canada notice.

Food and medicine first. We pull recalls from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency, Health Canada, and Transport Canada, then rank and reword them so you can see what matters at a glance.

CHECK IN SECONDS
Search any product, brand, or ingredient — “cheese,” “peanut,” your car’s make — and instantly see if there’s an active recall.

KNOW WHAT TO DO
Each recall opens to plain-language steps, who’s most at risk, where it was sold, and the official notice one tap away.

YOUR GUARDIAN — ALERTS FOR WHAT YOU OWN
Tell RecallGuardian the brands and categories your household cares about. It checks new recalls in the background and notifies you only on a match — and you choose how loud: Critical only, High & critical, or Everything. Scan a product’s barcode to add it in one tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No tracking. The recall data is public; your watchlist and the matching stay on your device.

RecallGuardian re-ranks and rewrites official recalls to make them useful. The Government of Canada notice is always the authoritative source — and it’s always one tap away.

Recall data: Government of Canada, Recalls and Safety Alerts (Open Government Licence – Canada).

## App Privacy (nutrition labels)
Answer in App Store Connect → App Privacy:
- **Data collected:** None. Select **“Data Not Collected.”**
- Rationale: no account/login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app fetches public recall data and (only when you scan) looks up a barcode via Open Food Facts; neither is linked to your identity. The watchlist and all matching stay on-device.

## App Review notes
RecallGuardian shows Canadian product-recall data from the Government of Canada “Recalls and Safety Alerts” open dataset (open.canada.ca, Open Government Licence). Optional barcode scanning looks up product names via Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). There is no account and no backend of ours — the household watchlist and all recall matching happen on-device. No demo account is required. Camera access is used only for optional barcode scanning; notifications are optional and used only to alert the user to recalls that match their own watchlist.
