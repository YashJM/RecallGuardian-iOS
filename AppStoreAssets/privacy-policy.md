# RecallGuardian — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

RecallGuardian is built to be private. We do not have accounts, we do not track
you, and we do not run a server that stores anything about you.

## What we collect
**Nothing about you.** RecallGuardian has no account or login, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, sell, or share personal
information.

## What stays on your device
Your **watchlist** (the brands, products, and categories you choose to watch) and
your **alert settings** are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded
to us or anyone else. New recalls are matched against your watchlist **on your
phone**.

## Network requests the app makes
To do its job, the app contacts two public services. Neither receives anything
that identifies you.

- **Government of Canada — Recalls and Safety Alerts** (recalls-rappels.canada.ca):
  to download the public recall data. This is a normal request for public
  information; we send no personal data.
- **Open Food Facts** (openfoodfacts.org), **only when you scan a barcode**: the
  scanned barcode number is sent to look up the product’s name. The barcode is
  not linked to you, and nothing else is sent.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** (optional): used only to alert you about recalls that match
  your own watchlist.
- **Camera** (optional): used only for barcode scanning to add a product to your
  watchlist. Images are processed on-device and are not stored or uploaded.

## Children
RecallGuardian does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Data source & disclaimer
Recall information comes from the Government of Canada “Recalls and Safety Alerts”
open data, used under the Open Government Licence – Canada. RecallGuardian
re-ranks and rewords recalls to make them easier to use; the official Government
of Canada notice is always the authoritative source and is linked on every
recall. RecallGuardian is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of
Canada.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and post the new version at
https://recallguardian.ca/privacy.
